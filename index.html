FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my capital locked for the full term? +
Your principal is locked to its plan until maturity, so it keeps compounding daily growth undisturbed. You can always start a new investment from your available balance.
Which currencies can I hold? +
Your wallet supports Naira (NGN), US Dollar (USD), Tether (USDT) and Bitcoin (BTC). You can convert between any of them at the live rate shown on your dashboard.
Do I need to verify my identity? +
Withdrawals require an approved KYC submission — a valid ID document plus a linked bank account. Deposits and investing don't require KYC.
How long do withdrawals take? +
Withdrawal requests are reviewed by our operations team and typically resolved within one business day. You'll see the status update on your Transactions page.
Is there a referral program? +
Yes — every account gets a referral code. Share it, and commissions earned by your referrals appear in your Commission Record.
